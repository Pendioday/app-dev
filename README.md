# Star Wars: Episode IV – A New Hope (1977)

**Genre:** Sci-Fi, Adventure, Fantasy  
**Director:** George Lucas

---

## Synopsis
Luke Skywalker, a young farm boy, discovers a message from Princess Leia hidden in a droid, leading him to join the Rebel Alliance against the tyrannical Galactic Empire.

---

### Main Characters & Roles
| Character | Actor | Role |
|-----------|-------|------|
| Luke Skywalker | Mark Hamill | Hero, Rebel fighter |
| Princess Leia | Carrie Fisher | Rebel leader, diplomat |
| Han Solo | Harrison Ford | Smuggler turned hero |
| Darth Vader | David Prowse / James Earl Jones | Sith Lord, enforcer of the Empire |
| Obi-Wan Kenobi | Alec Guinness | Mentor to Luke, Jedi Master |

---

### Highlights
- Luke discovers his destiny and the Force  
- Rescue of Princess Leia from the Death Star  
- Destruction of the Death Star

---

### Why It Stands Out
- Strong hero’s journey narrative  
- Iconic characters and memorable dialogue  
- Groundbreaking special effects for its time

---

### Trivia
- Originally released simply as *Star Wars*, later renamed *Episode IV – A New Hope*  
- Won 6 Academy Awards, including Best Visual Effects
